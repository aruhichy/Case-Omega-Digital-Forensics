## 2. Purpose of Evidence Identification

The purpose of evidence identification is to identify potential digital evidence sources that may help investigators understand the suspected ransomware incident at OmegaCare Multispeciality Hospital.

The investigation focuses on identifying affected systems, examining system and network activities, understanding possible unauthorized access, and reconstructing the sequence of events.

All evidence described in this academic project is fictional and intended for educational simulation only. No real hospital systems or patient information are involved.

## 6. Evidence Handling Precautions

The following precautions must be followed during evidence collection:

1. Do not modify or delete original evidence.
2. Record the evidence ID and source asset for every item.
3. Record the collection date and time in IST.
4. Maintain a chain-of-custody record for every acquired item.
5. Calculate SHA-256 hashes for acquired evidence files wherever applicable.
6. Store acquired evidence securely with restricted access.
7. Perform examination on verified forensic copies whenever possible.
8. Document every action performed during the investigation.

## 7. Limitations

- This is a fictional academic investigation.
- No actual hospital systems, patient records, or organizational logs were accessed.
- The evidence sources listed above are planned or simulated.
- The presence of an evidence source does not establish that it contains proof of ransomware activity.
- Any conclusions must be based on the evidence actually created and examined during the project.
