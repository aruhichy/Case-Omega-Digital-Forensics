## 🧩 Project Overview

**Case Omega** is an academic digital forensics project that demonstrates the investigation of a fictional ransomware incident affecting a healthcare network.

The project follows a structured forensic workflow, from first responder documentation and evidence identification to integrity verification, safe malware-behaviour simulation, timeline reconstruction, and final forensic reporting.

The objective is to understand how digital evidence can be documented, preserved, analysed, and presented during a cybercrime investigation.

> ⚠️ **Academic Simulation:** OmegaCare Multispeciality Hospital, the incident, logs, and evidence records are fictional or synthetically generated. No real hospital systems or patient data were accessed.

## 🎯 Investigation Objectives

- Document the incident scenario and first responder procedures.
- Identify potential digital evidence and maintain evidence records.
- Demonstrate SHA-256 hash generation and integrity verification.
- Analyse simulated ransomware-related behaviour safely.
- Correlate synthetic events into a chronological timeline.
- Prepare a structured digital forensic investigation report.

## 🧪 Key Project Components

### 1. Evidence Integrity Verification
Implemented SHA-256 hashing to generate evidence hashes and verify file integrity against recorded baseline values.

### 2. Safe Ransomware Behaviour Simulator
Created a Python-based educational simulator that examines demonstration files and generates a harmless simulated ransom note and activity log.

The simulator does **not** encrypt, delete, or overwrite files.

### 3. Timeline Reconstruction
Processed synthetic event records, sorted events chronologically, generated a correlated timeline, and created a visual timeline chart.

### 4. Forensic Documentation
Prepared incident documentation, evidence records, chain-of-custody documentation, analysis findings, investigation limitations, and a final forensic report.

## 📊 Timeline Visualisation

The project includes a timeline chart illustrating the chronological order of six synthetic events from the fictional incident.

The sequence represents simulated observations and does not independently establish causation or identify an attacker.

## 📄 Final Report

The final forensic report includes:

- Case information and executive summary
- Investigation objectives and methodology
- Evidence collection and integrity verification
- Malware analysis and simulated indicators
- Timeline analysis and key findings
- Recommendations and investigation limitations

## 🔐 Safety & Ethical Considerations

This project was developed for educational purposes in a controlled environment.

- All incident data is fictional or synthetic.
- The simulator is non-destructive.
- No real patient information was used.
- No unauthorised systems were accessed.
- No actual ransomware was deployed.
- The project does not claim to represent a legally admissible real-world forensic investigation.
